# Answers from David and Steve (26 Oct 2026, evening)

Replies to the questions sent on 26 Oct. Their words are kept close; my reading is marked "Reading".

## Roster

1. **Shift stage colours (the ribbon)**, for the roster and allocations:
   - Unfilled shift: **grey**
   - New shift (offered or broadcast, nobody yet): **pale yellow**
   - Allocated, not accepted: **darker yellow**
   - Accepted: **pink**
   - Worker has pulled out: **purple**
   - Booked: **green**
   - Cancelled: **red**
   Reading: grey = nothing done yet; pale yellow = offered or broadcast; darker yellow = someone assigned, waiting for them; pink = accepted but not yet booked (used when the business wants to approve, e.g. agency nominations); green = booked; purple = the person withdrew and the shift needs filling again.
2. **Template.** The template is the ideal week. Clients pick a template, or duplicate the previous period (a week or a month).
3. **The "T" line** under the name is always the person being covered (e.g. "Sick leave – Sandra Harrison").
4. **Shift letters.** A mindset, not a fixed rule: teachers and labourers work day / afternoon / night; nurses think AM / PM / NS. Each business sets its own shift types: letter, name and standard hours (e.g. D, Day shift, 7:00–3:30). Shifts that cross pay rates matter later for finance and payroll; allow for it.
5. **After publishing.** Changes only as a logged change, by someone authorised. Changes are requested and approved. Option to turn roster changes off. Optional shift swaps agreed between workers in the app. **All of these are settings each client chooses.**
6. **Priority ("Pri")**: later, in more detail.

## Agencies and clients

7. **Employer and agency in one business: yes.** Agencies are always employers too (internal, casual and bank staff).
8. **Agency fill timing** depends on urgency; set by the client, with defaults and room for more rules. As given: "If a shift is within two hours (10 minutes) for the same day it would be (2 hours), next day or later (2 hours)."
   **Confirmed by David (26 Oct):** defaults per agency before the shift moves to the next one: starts within 2 hours → **10 minutes**; later the same day → **1 hour**; next day or later → **2 hours**. Each client can change these and add their own rules.
9. **What clients see of agency workers:** everything the agency sees except contracts and agreements with the agency, and it varies by the client user's role (a nurse in charge sees AHPRA registration and supporting documents, not the signed contract). Build: a list of all document types, and for each, who can see it (agency roles, employer roles).
10. **Agency nominations:** the client chooses: accept automatically (e.g. urgent shifts) or approve first (more notice). Adjustable per client.
11. **Clients not on CROP: yes,** a light account to order shifts.
12. **Clients page stays for everyone.** Small businesses have clients too (a contractor sending staff to jobs, e.g. servicing a real estate agency's properties).
