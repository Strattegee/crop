# 5. ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY — DRAFT

Applies to all administrators and mobile worker users with access to CROPwms. Customer must make this policy accessible to its Authorised Users.

- Use CROPwms only for legitimate business or employment-related purposes and within assigned access permissions.

- Do not access another person’s account or records without authorisation, share passwords or bypass identity and security controls.

- Do not upload malicious code; attack, scrape, overload or interfere with the Services; or attempt unauthorised reverse engineering.

- Do not use the system to harass, discriminate against, unlawfully surveil or retaliate against workers, or to conceal or falsify required employment records.

- Do not upload unlawful content, unnecessary sensitive information or information that the organisation has no lawful reason to collect.

- Report suspected security incidents, wrongly assigned records or accidental access to another organisation’s data promptly to \[SECURITY CONTACT\].

- CROPwms may proportionately restrict problematic activity where necessary for safety or lawfulness, with notice and opportunity to remedy where practicable.
