# 1. Who we are

## About CROPwms

CROPwms provides workforce management software for organisations and a mobile application for their workers. This policy explains how we handle personal information both for our own business operations and when providing software to employer customers. Employers may also have their own privacy notices and legal obligations.

# 2. What information we collect

## From businesses and administrators

We may collect name, work contact information, organisation details, account preferences, subscription records, communications, payment status and service support information. Where a business on CROPwms offers a public page for new customers to request staff, we hold what a customer sends through it (their organisation, name, email address, phone number, the address of the site and the work they need) for that business, which decides whether to take them on. Card details may be handled by \[PAYMENT PROCESSOR — CONFIRM\] rather than stored by CROPwms.

## From and about workers

Depending on features enabled by their employer, we may handle work contact details, home or postal addresses (recorded by the employer's office; a worker may have several with one marked current, and earlier addresses and corrections are kept on file rather than overwritten; from the current address's postcode CROPwms works out an approximate straight-line distance to a site, to help the employer offer nearby shifts — it is shown to the employer's office users only as a number of kilometres and to the worker on shifts offered to them; beyond a distance the employer chooses, people who live further away are listed after nearer people who are just as suitable, but it never stops anyone being offered or booked, and nothing about it is stored or shared; postcode locations come from GeoNames, www.geonames.org, CC BY 4.0), employment role, workplace, roster and shift details, availability, attendance and timesheet information, licences or credentials (which can include checks such as police checks and vaccination records where the employer requires them), right to work details (citizenship or visa status, visa expiry and any work conditions), gender (female or male, optional, recorded by an employer so it can meet a client's request for a worker of a particular gender, for example in personal care), date of birth (optional, visible only to the employer's office users, used to show when a worker is under 18 so the employer can take care with work it has marked as 18 and over or late-night work; it never decides who works a shift and is never shown in the app, sent in messages or shared with another business), documents uploaded by the employer (including a CV, a signed contract or another document its office keeps on a worker's record; a CV sent with an online application is kept there too), through the app or through a one-off upload link the employer sends, messages, a log of phone calls the employer's office records with them (when, who in the office, the outcome such as "said yes" or "no answer", and an optional short note; CROPwms does not make or record the calls themselves) and audit history. Where an employer opens an online application form on CROPwms, we hold what a job applicant sends through it for that employer: their name, email address, mobile number, suburb and postcode, the roles and kind of work they want, their usual availability, whether they say they have the right to work and their status (citizen, permanent resident or visa holder; no document numbers or visa details), whether they have a driver's licence and a car, the qualifications they say they hold with any photos or files of them, a CV, a short note about themselves, how they heard about the employer, their answers to the employer's own questions, and when they agreed to this policy and the worker privacy notice (and which version). An application is held unconfirmed until the applicant opens the confirmation link we email them (within 48 hours); only then does the employer see it, and unconfirmed applications are deleted once the link has run out. To stop automated spam we keep a scrambled (keyed hash) form of the applicant's IP address for a short time to limit how many applications come from one connection, never the address itself, and where the employer has switched it on, Cloudflare Turnstile checks the form was filled in by a person (see Schedule D). The employer checks the applicant's right to work and qualifications itself before they work. We may collect device identifiers, app version, authentication, diagnostic, security and push notification tokens required to operate and protect the mobile app. We do not collect GPS location, biometrics, banking or payroll data. Vaccination records are health information: they are recorded only where an employer requires them for the work, are visible only to that employer's authorised office users (a supervisor, described below, sees only whether a vaccination record is held and current, not which vaccination), and are never included in emails or text messages.

# 4. Sharing, overseas access and storage

## Disclosure

Information may be available to the relevant employer and authorised users according to roles (an employer may limit some of its office users to chosen sites or projects, and may give some a supervisor role: a supervisor sees, for the sites or projects they are given, workers' names, roles, credentials and licences (type, issuer, dates and whether checked), whether right to work is met, the person's work summary and their shifts there, but not contact details, addresses (only the approximate distance from a worker's home postcode to a site), date of birth, right to work details, employment records, notes, documents, call logs or audit history), our employees with a legitimate support/security need, contracted hosting and technical service providers, professional advisers and authorities where lawfully required. Where a staffing agency using CROPwms supplies a worker to a business it is linked with, the agency puts the worker forward and CROPwms gives that business the worker’s name, the agency’s name and the shift (and, if the agency later takes the worker off the shift, any reason the agency gives); the agency checks the worker against the business’s requirements and only the result is shared (including which of the business’s listed credentials the worker held on the day, by name, so they count towards its site cover and equipment checks), not the worker’s credentials, documents or contact details. When a business emails a shift to a staffing agency that does not use CROPwms, the email gives the shift, its requirements and the sending user’s name and work email for the reply; it contains no worker information. If that agency replies with a worker, the business may record the worker’s name against the agency and the shift (name only). When a business that supplies workers to its own clients records a client’s order in CROPwms and a worker is booked on it, CROPwms can email the person at the client who placed the order the worker’s name (the first name they go by and their last name), the role and the shift (site, area, date and times); nothing else about the worker is included. CROPwms keeps that client contact’s name, email and phone number with the order, and the business may save its clients’ contacts (name, position, email, phone number and a short note) to pick when taking later orders; a saved contact can be switched off but orders keep the details they were taken with. The business may also log the phone calls its office records with a client's contacts (when, who, the outcome and an optional short note). We do not sell workers’ personal information.
