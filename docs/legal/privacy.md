# 1. Who we are

## About CROPwms

CROPwms provides workforce management software for organisations and a mobile application for their workers. This policy explains how we handle personal information both for our own business operations and when providing software to employer customers. Employers may also have their own privacy notices and legal obligations.

# 2. What information we collect

## From businesses and administrators

We may collect name, work contact information, organisation details, account preferences, subscription records, communications, payment status and service support information. Card details may be handled by \[PAYMENT PROCESSOR — CONFIRM\] rather than stored by CROPwms.

## From and about workers

Depending on features enabled by their employer, we may handle work contact details, employment role, workplace, roster and shift details, availability, attendance and timesheet information, licences or credentials (which can include checks such as police checks and vaccination records where the employer requires them), right to work details (citizenship or visa status, visa expiry and any work conditions), documents uploaded by the employer or through the app, messages and audit history. We may collect device identifiers, app version, authentication, diagnostic, security and push notification tokens required to operate and protect the mobile app. We do not collect GPS location, biometrics, banking or payroll data. Vaccination records are health information: they are recorded only where an employer requires them for the work, are visible only to that employer's authorised office users, and are never included in emails or text messages.

## Sources

We obtain information directly from account users, employers who create and maintain workforce records, authorised integrations and service providers. Where legally required, collection notices will identify when information is collected from someone other than the individual.

# 3. Why we use information

## Purposes

We use personal information to operate and secure accounts, display employer-authorised shifts and workplace records, provide notifications, maintain logs, answer support requests, administer business subscriptions, prevent misuse, meet legal obligations and improve reliability using appropriately limited technical diagnostics.

## Marketing

We do not use employer-provided workforce records to market unrelated products to workers. Where we send commercial electronic messages, we will follow applicable consent and unsubscribe requirements.

# 4. Sharing, overseas access and storage

## Disclosure

Information may be available to the relevant employer and authorised users according to roles, our employees with a legitimate support/security need, contracted hosting and technical service providers, professional advisers and authorities where lawfully required. Where a staffing agency using CROPwms supplies a worker to a business it is linked with, the agency puts the worker forward and CROPwms gives that business the worker’s name, the agency’s name and the shift; the agency checks the worker against the business’s requirements and only the result is shared, not the worker’s credentials, documents or contact details. We do not sell workers’ personal information.
