# 7. LAUNCH DECISIONS AND IMPLEMENTATION REQUIREMENTS

## Blocking before any live worker personal information

- Insert the existing Australian company’s exact legal name, ACN/ABN, business address, legal/privacy/security contacts and support channels.

- Produce a full data inventory and privacy impact assessment: exact fields, source, purpose, employer permissions, retention, sensitive-data flags and cross-border flows.

- Decide whether the product includes shift rostering alone or attendance, messaging, credentials, location, health, payroll, biometrics, AI ranking or automated decisions; enable only reviewed modules.

- Confirm actual Australian cloud regions, backup regions, push messaging services, identity vendors, payment provider, analytics/crash tools, support tools and every subprocessor with country/access exposure.

- Complete app-specific permission and collection screens; provide APP 5 collection notice where required and separate employer notices, not just terms acceptance.

- Implement secure access control, tenancy isolation, audit trails, export, retention and incident response before handling worker records.

- Confirm pricing, seat definitions, GST, trials, billing frequency, renewal notices, cancellation timing and refund flow before taking paid subscriptions.

- Choose real SLA and support targets after measuring infrastructure and staffing. Complete cyber/professional indemnity insurance and set liability caps accordingly.

- Check whether workers under 18 can use the mobile app and implement age-appropriate privacy practices.

- Have an Australian commercial technology/privacy lawyer approve the contract suite and state-specific surveillance/monitoring requirements before publication.

## Conditions that trigger additional legal work

- If GPS/geofencing or attendance tracking is added: implement purpose limitation, notice, mobile permission controls and jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction worker-surveillance review.

- If health records, medical leave reasons, biometric ID, police checks or other sensitive information are added: do a fresh PIA and assess collection-consent and sector-specific requirements.

- If payroll calculations, award interpretation or employee-status decisions are added: assess Fair Work compliance, auditability, reliance warnings and human review.

- If AI recommends shifts, ranks workers or materially informs employment decisions: assess fairness, transparency, error correction and APP 1.7–1.9 disclosure duties beginning 10 December 2026.

- If international customers or overseas hosting are added: localise privacy notices and contracts, assess GDPR/UK GDPR and relevant transfer mechanisms as applicable; Australian drafting is not sufficient.

- If the worker app offers optional paid digital services directly to individuals: revisit app-store billing rules and consumer terms; employer-paid B2B access is a different arrangement.

## Practical publication/acceptance process

- Use a clear online order showing total fees and subscription measurements before contract acceptance.

- Record accepting person, organisation, UTC timestamp, version hash/URL and copy of the agreed Order.

- Link all incorporated schedules from the checkout and administrator onboarding screens.

- Provide mobile workers the shorter notice at onboarding, with accessible full policy and employer notice links.

- Maintain a historic version archive; alert customers before material term or price changes.

- Provide one self-service cancellation flow and a separate secure export/download flow.
