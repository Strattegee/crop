# Australian-first SaaS legal drafting pack

Version 0.1 \| 24 October 2026 \| For legal and product review

Assumed model: existing Australian company; initially Australian business customers; employee-facing mobile app; Australian-hosted primary customer data; pricing and enabled workforce features to be decided; potential international expansion later.

IMPORTANT: These are substantive working drafts, not an opinion that the documents are legally complete or suitable for immediate publication. Bracketed fields and expressly conditional provisions must be resolved. The promises about service levels, security, data locations, retention and subprocessors must be validated against the real product before signing customers.
