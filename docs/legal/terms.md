# 1. Definitions and contract structure

## 1.1 Core terms

“Authorised User” means an individual to whom Customer grants access, including administrators and workers using the mobile app. “Customer Data” means information Customer or its Authorised Users submit to or generate within the Services on Customer’s behalf, excluding Provider account, billing, security and operational data created for Provider’s own purposes. “Services” means the subscribed CROPwms functionality specified on the Order. “Order” means the accepted online or signed subscription order identifying the customer, features, term, fees and usage limits.

## 1.2 Contract hierarchy

The Order, this Agreement and its incorporated Data Processing Agreement (DPA), Acceptable Use Policy, Service Level Schedule and Security/Data Lifecycle Schedule together form the Contract. If provisions conflict, the DPA prevails for processing personal information; the Order prevails for expressly negotiated commercial details; otherwise these Terms prevail. Nothing overrides applicable law.

## 1.3 Business use

Customer confirms it is acquiring the Services for business purposes and has authority to bind its organisation. Worker app users are not required to purchase subscriptions; the employer is the contractual customer. Workers receive a separate privacy notice and relevant app-use conditions.

# 2. Services and account administration

## 2.1 Access licence

During an active subscription, Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use the Services internally for its lawful workforce operations and to permit Authorised Users to access the relevant functions. Customer may not resell or sublicense the Services without written permission.

## 2.2 Features and changes

Subscribed features and limits appear in the Order. Provider may improve or modify the Services, but must not materially reduce core paid functionality during an active prepaid term without a reasonably equivalent replacement or the Customer’s right to terminate the affected portion and receive a pro-rata refund for unused prepaid fees. Security-critical changes may be made promptly with notice as soon as practicable.

## 2.3 Accounts

Customer controls which staff may access its workspace and their assigned roles. Provider is responsible for the platform’s identity controls as documented; Customer is responsible for its role assignments, timely removal of access and reasonable safeguarding of its credentials. Each party promptly notifies the other of suspected account compromise.

## 2.4 Worker app

The worker-facing mobile app is an access channel to the Customer’s workforce workspace. Provider will distinguish its own app account information from employment records controlled by Customer. No worker payment is required unless a separately disclosed consumer product is introduced under separate terms.

# 3. Customer responsibilities and employment compliance

## 3.1 Lawful use

Customer must use the Services lawfully; provide appropriate worker collection notices; obtain permissions where required for monitoring, location, identity checks or sensitive information; and not upload data it has no right to use.

## 3.2 Employer decisions

Customer is solely responsible for wages, awards, classifications, employment contracts, rosters, staffing and legal decisions it makes, and for reviewing outputs before relying on them. Provider is responsible for the Services meeting its contractual specifications and must not disclaim responsibility for errors caused by its own breach.

## 3.3 Record preservation

Customer is responsible for determining applicable legal record-retention obligations. Provider shall provide the export and transition mechanisms described in the Data Lifecycle Schedule and shall not deliberately destroy Customer Data in contravention of an agreed preservation hold or applicable law.

# 4. Fees, renewals and cancellation

## 4.1 Fees

The Order specifies fees, currency (AUD unless otherwise stated), billing basis, applicable GST, included users/features, usage measurement and invoicing/payment method. No undisclosed seat minimum or overage charges apply. The customer can review its usage and projected charges before changes take effect.

## 4.2 Subscription term

Unless an Order expressly provides otherwise, subscriptions renew month-to-month and may be cancelled before the next renewal date, taking effect at the end of the current paid period. No long-term commitment arises merely from using the worker app.

## 4.3 Fee changes

Provider may alter the price of future monthly periods on at least 30 days’ advance notice, with a right to cancel before the increase applies. Fixed-term prepaid fees cannot increase during that term without Customer’s agreement.

## 4.4 Failed payments

If a payment is overdue, Provider will provide written notice and a reasonable period of at least 14 days to resolve it before ordinary non-critical access is suspended, except for fraud or significant security risk. Customer retains reasonable access to export employment records during a payment dispute, subject to proportionate safeguards.

## 4.5 Refunds

Prepaid fees are ordinarily non-refundable for Customer’s convenience cancellation during the paid period, but Provider will provide refunds or other remedies required by law and pro-rata refunds where expressly stated in this Agreement, including certain Provider breach or material degradation events.

Provider and licensors retain all rights in the platform, software, documentation and improvements. Customer retains its rights in Customer Data. ## 7.1 Suspension

Provider may suspend affected access when reasonably necessary to respond to material security threats, unlawful use or substantial breach, using the least disruptive practicable measure.
