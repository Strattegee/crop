# 4. MOBILE WORKER PRIVACY NOTICE — DRAFT

## Who operates this app?

CROPwms is provided by Strattegee Pty Ltd. Your employer uses it to manage certain work information. Your employer decides what employment records to enter and which authorised people at work can see them.

## What information might appear?

Your name, work contact details, workplace or role, shifts and rosters, availability, attendance or timesheets, and credentials may appear if your employer has enabled those features. The app also uses limited login, security, diagnostic and notification information to work properly.

## Who sees it?

Your employer’s authorised managers and other approved people can access information according to their permissions. CROPwms personnel and service providers may access limited information when needed to operate, secure or support the service. Other workers should only see information necessary for their work role. If you work through an agency and it puts you forward for a shift at a business it supplies, that business sees your name, your agency and the shift (and, if your agency takes you off the shift, any reason it gives); your agency checks your credentials against the business’s requirements (the business sees which of its listed credentials you held on the day, by name only) and your documents and contact details stay with your agency.

## What does the app not mean?

Installing the app does not itself mean that you consent to location tracking, camera monitoring, biometric checks, new employment conditions or marketing. If such features are introduced, you should receive an explanation and any legally required notice or choice before they are used.

## Your choices and help

For an incorrect shift or employment record, contact your employer. For app account, privacy or security issues, contact info@strattegee.com. You can read CROPwms’s full Privacy Policy at cropwms.com/legal/privacy and your employer’s notice at \[EMPLOYER-SUPPLIED LINK\]. You may have legal rights to access or correct personal information held about you.

## Out-of-hours notifications

The app may send shift-related notices. Receiving a notification does not, by itself, alter your employment rights or require a response outside working hours. Any legal right to disconnect continues to apply.
