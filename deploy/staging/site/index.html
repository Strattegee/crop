Run your whole operation from one roster

CROP rosters your staff, your equipment and your consumables together, and checks that everyone on a shift is qualified to be there.

Restaurants, construction and equipment hire, traffic management, healthcare, warehousing: if your business runs on shifts, CROP is built for you. Plan the week, publish it to your team's phones, and fill gaps fast when someone calls in sick.

For employers

Staff

Roster full-time, part-time, casual and relief staff by day, week or month, by area or by person. See everyone's hours at a glance and publish changes instantly.

Equipment

Book machines and vehicles to shifts the same way you book people. No double bookings, registration and service dates tracked, and only licensed operators assigned to each machine.

Consumables

Issue PPE, fuel and supplies against a shift, a job or a person. Know what each shift really costs, and what to charge for it.

Compliance on every shift

Set the licences, tickets, checks and training each role needs. CROP keeps track of expiry dates, reminds people before anything runs out, and won't let you roster someone who isn't eligible on the day. Right to work and visa hour limits are checked too.

Fill gaps in minutes

When a shift opens up, CROP shows you who in your own team is available and qualified. Still short? Press Agency fill and the shift goes to the agencies you work with, in the order you prefer. Agency staff must meet exactly the same requirements as your own.

For staffing and labour hire agencies

CROP's allocation screen shows every open request across all your clients alongside every available, compliant person in your pool. Receive shifts straight from employers who use CROP, take orders from clients who don't, and offer work to your people's phones. The first to accept gets the shift.

For workers

Your employer or agency will invite you to CROP. Use the CROP app on your phone to see your shifts, accept offers, set your availability and keep your licences and documents up to date. Sign in with the email address your employer or agency has on file.

Your data stays in Australia

CROP is hosted in Sydney. Every business's information is kept separate, sign-in uses two-factor authentication, and documents open only through short-lived private links.
