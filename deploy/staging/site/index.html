CROP Workforce Management

Credentials, rostering, operations and people, in one place for staffing agencies.

CROP helps agencies roster their staff, fill casual shifts, and keep every worker's registrations, checks and training up to date. Workers see and accept shifts in the CROP app on their phone.

For workers

Your agency will invite you to CROP. Download the CROP app when it's available, and sign in with the email address your agency has on file.

Contact

CROP is in early access with selected agencies. To talk to us, email info@strattegee.com.
